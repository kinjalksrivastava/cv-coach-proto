### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Rework your experience bullets to focus on what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered—highlighting the transferable skills you used. Where you have a concrete result or outcome, include it, but the main aim is to show your skills in action.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  For each experience bullet, clarify the skill you demonstrated and the impact of your work. Add outcomes or changes where you can genuinely evidence them, but do not invent figures just to fill the space.
- 🟠 **There's a paragraph at the top with no heading**  
  If this is your profile, add a heading such as 'Profile' or 'Summary' so it is clearly identified. A profile works best as about three sentences on what you bring and where you are heading.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., '09/2023 – 06/2024'). This helps a reader distinguish between short and long roles, and avoids assumptions about the duration.
- 🟠 **Education comes after work experience**  
  For a student CV, education usually comes first unless you have several years of directly relevant work. Consider moving your education section up unless your work experience is the main focus for your applications.
