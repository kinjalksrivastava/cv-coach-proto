## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV presents a clear and consistent career history, with well-defined roles and responsibilities across a long and relevant professional timeline. The section order and structure are logical for a mature candidate with significant experience. However, there are some areas where you could add detail to help a recruiter understand your specific contributions and skills, especially in your education and experience sections. This feedback focuses on structure and completeness, as no target role or job description was provided. Nothing on your CV currently refers to AI, machine learning, or related tools—if you have relevant experience or coursework in this area, consider adding it.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Your degrees have nothing under them but a grade and a date**  
  Add a line or two under each degree where you genuinely have something to share: relevant modules, a thesis and what it was about, an exchange semester, certificates, scholarships, or mentoring programmes. Only include what is true and relevant to your future applications.
- 🟠 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Rework the duty-only bullets in your experience section to show what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered—highlighting the transferable skills you used or developed.
- 🟠 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  For each experience bullet, clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, and identify the transferable skill it demonstrates. Add results where you genuinely have them, but only if appropriate and accurate.
- 🟠 **Education comes after work experience**  
  For a student CV, education usually comes first unless you have extensive, directly relevant work experience. Consider whether your current section order best supports your future applications.
- 🟡 **Interests are listed without any detail**  
  Add detail to your interests—what kind, how often, and to what level—to give a clearer picture of your personal pursuits and commitments.

_Worked examples of weaker and stronger bullet points — Career Services' own, not rewrites of your CV — can be opened underneath this report._
