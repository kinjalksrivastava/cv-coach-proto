### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree in your education section**  
  Currently, your education entries list only the degree, institution, and dates. Add a line or two under each degree where you have relevant content: key modules, thesis topic and what you actually did, exchange semesters, certificates, scholarships, or mentoring programmes. Only include what is true and relevant to your future applications.
- 🔴 **Rework experience bullets to show your contribution and skills**  
  Many of your experience bullets describe tasks or duties without showing what you personally contributed, how you did it, or which transferable skills you developed. For each bullet, clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered—highlighting the skills you gained or demonstrated.
- 🔴 **Add outcomes or results to experience bullets where possible**  
  Your experience bullets currently focus on tasks rather than outcomes or changes. Where appropriate and accurate, add what changed as a result of your work—such as improvements, efficiencies, or skills gained. Only include outcomes you can genuinely support.
- 🟠 **Add months to all date ranges**  
  Several entries show only years. Add the month to each date range (e.g., '09/2023 – 06/2024') so a reader can see the true duration of each role or activity.
- 🟠 **Make bullet punctuation consistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it consistently throughout your CV.
