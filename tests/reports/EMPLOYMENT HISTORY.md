## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV presents a substantial and varied professional background, with clear role titles and descriptions for each position. The structure is generally strong, and your education and additional activities are well documented. However, most of your experience bullets focus on duties rather than highlighting your specific contributions or the skills you demonstrated, which makes it harder for a recruiter to see your impact. No content currently refers to AI or AI tools—if you have relevant experience or skills in this area, consider adding them. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; for tailored advice, please share your intended position or industry.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Revise your experience bullets to focus on what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, highlighting the transferable skills you used or developed. This will help recruiters understand your impact and suitability for future roles.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  For each experience bullet, clarify the skill you demonstrated and, where possible, include a concrete result or change. This makes your achievements and strengths more visible to the reader.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., '09/2023 – 06/2024') so that readers can accurately assess the duration of your roles and studies.
- 🟠 **Bullet punctuation is inconsistent**  
  Choose a consistent punctuation style for your bullets—either all with or all without full stops—and apply it throughout your CV.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within EMPLOYMENT HISTORY**  
  Use past tense for completed roles and present tense for your current position, and keep this consistent within the section to maintain clarity and professionalism.

_Worked examples of weaker and stronger bullet points — Career Services' own, not rewrites of your CV — can be opened underneath this report._
