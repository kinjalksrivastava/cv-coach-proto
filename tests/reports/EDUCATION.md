### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Your degrees have nothing under them but a grade and a date**  
  A reader learns which certificate you hold and nothing about what you did. Add a line or two under each degree where you genuinely have something: the modules that matter for the role you want, a thesis and what it was actually about, an exchange semester, a certificate or additional qualification, a scholarship, or a mentoring programme. Only include what is true and relevant to where you are applying.
- 🔴 **Entries in "PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE" have no description**  
  One of your experience entries (Medtech AG, St. Gallen, Switzerland 06/2008 – 06/2019) has no bullets or description. Add a few bullets under each entry covering what was done, how, and what changed as a result—focused on the transferable skills that matter.
- 🟠 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Some bullets only state the task or duty. Rework these to show what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered—highlighting the transferable skill demonstrated. Add a result where you genuinely have one.
- 🟠 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  Most experience bullets describe what was done but not the outcome or skill shown. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, and add a result if appropriate and accurate.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within "PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE"**  
  Tenses switch between present and past within the section. Use past tense for completed roles and present tense for your current position, and keep this consistent throughout.
