## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is concise and easy to follow, but it currently lacks the detail and evidence a recruiter needs to understand your background and skills. At the moment, each section contains only basic information such as dates, titles, and a grade, without any description of what you actually did or achieved. This makes it difficult for a reader to assess your experience or potential fit for a role. Since you have not provided a target job or industry, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; if you add a target, I can give more tailored advice. Nothing on your CV currently mentions experience with AI—if you have relevant coursework, projects, or skills in this area, consider adding them.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Your degrees have nothing under them but a grade and a date**  
  Add a line or two under each degree describing relevant modules, a thesis (with what you actually did), an exchange semester, certificates, scholarships, or mentoring programmes—only what is true and relevant to your future applications.
- 🔴 **No bullet points anywhere in the CV**  
  Include bullet points under each role and activity to explain what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result. This gives recruiters the evidence they need to understand your skills and contributions.
- 🔴 **Entries in "Work experience" have no description**  
  Add a few bullet points under each work experience entry, focusing on your responsibilities, how you approached them, and the outcomes—especially those that demonstrate transferable skills.
- 🔴 **The CV does not fill a page**  
  There is significant space to add more detail about your experiences, achievements, and skills. A fuller page gives a more complete picture of your background.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., 'Sep 2023 – Jun 2024') so a reader can accurately understand the duration of your experiences.
