## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is well-structured and covers a broad range of academic, professional, and extracurricular experiences, which gives a recruiter a good overview of your background. The work experience section is particularly clear, with each entry showing both your role and a description of your responsibilities. However, there are several areas where more detail or clarification would help a reader understand your achievements and skills, especially in your education and extracurricular sections. Nothing on your CV currently refers to AI experience—if you have worked with AI tools or completed relevant coursework or projects, consider adding this. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; for tailored advice, please share your intended role or industry.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree in Education**  
  Currently, some degrees only show the title and dates, which tells a reader little about your academic focus or achievements. Add lines under each degree to highlight relevant modules, thesis topics, exchanges, certificates, or other experiences that matter for your future applications.
- 🔴 **State your role titles in Volunteering, Societies & Interests**  
  Some entries in this section list only the organisation or event, not your specific role. Make sure to state what function you held so a reader can understand your level of responsibility and involvement.
- 🔴 **Develop or remove the nearly empty 'Interessen' section**  
  The 'Interessen' section currently contains almost no content. Either add a few interests or hobbies that reflect your personal pursuits, or remove the heading to avoid drawing attention to the gap.
- 🔴 **Make experience bullets outcome-focused**  
  Most of your experience bullets describe tasks rather than the skills you used or the results you achieved. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, adding outcomes or skills where appropriate.
- 🟠 **Make bullet punctuation consistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it throughout your CV for a more professional appearance.
