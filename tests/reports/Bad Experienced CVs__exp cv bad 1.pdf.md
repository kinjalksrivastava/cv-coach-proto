### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **No bullet points anywhere in the CV**  
  Every role and activity needs a few lines saying what was actually done. Without them, a reader has only job titles to go on—add bullet points to each entry to clarify your responsibilities and achievements.
- 🔴 **Entries in 'BERUFLICHE ERFAHRUNG' have no description**  
  Add a few bullets under each experience entry covering what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result, focusing on transferable skills relevant to your future goals.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., '09/2023 – 06/2024') so a reader can distinguish between short and long experiences.
- 🟠 **Education comes after work experience**  
  For a student CV, education normally comes first unless you have several years of directly relevant work experience. Consider moving your education section to the top.
- 🟡 **Languages are listed without a level**  
  Add a plain label (e.g., Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Fluent) to each language, and consider using CEFR levels (A1–C2) for clarity.
