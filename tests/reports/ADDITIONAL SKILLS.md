### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Entries in "PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE" have no description**  
  One of your roles in the professional experience section has no bullets or description. Add a few bullets under each entry, focusing on what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result, especially highlighting transferable skills.
- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Some bullets in your professional experience section only state tasks or responsibilities. Rework these to show your specific actions, how you approached them, and the skills you demonstrated, with results where possible.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within "PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE"**  
  There are inconsistencies in verb tense within your professional experience section. Use present tense for your current role and past tense for previous roles, and keep this consistent throughout.
- 🟠 **Education comes after work experience**  
  For most student CVs, education is listed before work experience unless you have several years of directly relevant work. Consider whether your experience justifies the current order or if moving education up would be clearer.
- 🟡 **Interests are listed as single words**  
  Single-word interests are acceptable, but if one or two could say something interesting about you or show a skill, consider adding a short phrase for context. This is optional and not needed for every interest.
