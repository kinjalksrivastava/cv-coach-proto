### 2. CV format check

| Check | Status | Comment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 🟢 Good | 1 page — appropriate for a student or early-career CV. |
| Estimated ATS compatibility | 🟠 Needs attention | Standard fonts, but section headers some parsers may not recognise ("Academic Experience") — conventional wording such as "Work Experience" or "Education" parses more reliably; unusual bullet or icon characters (■). |
| Spelling and consistency | 🟠 Needs attention | Typos: no obvious typos in headings or dates. Spelling: British and American spellings are both used (programme/program) — pick one and keep it consistent. |

_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree in Academic Experience**  
  Currently, some education entries only show the certificate, grade, and dates. Add a line or two under each degree where you have relevant content: key modules, thesis details, exchange semesters, certificates, scholarships, or participation in programmes like the HSG Mentoring Programme. Only include what is true and relevant to your future applications.
- 🟠 **Expand experience bullets to show skills and outcomes**  
  Many experience bullets describe only the task. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, highlighting the transferable skills you developed. Where you have a measurable result or outcome, include it if appropriate and accurate.
- 🟡 **Make bullet punctuation consistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it consistently throughout your CV.

_Worked examples of weaker and stronger bullet points — Career Services' own, not rewrites of your CV — can be opened underneath this report._
