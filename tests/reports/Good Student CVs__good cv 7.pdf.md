## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is well-structured and concise, fitting comfortably on one page and providing clear role titles and descriptions for each professional experience. Grades are presented with their maximums, making them easy to interpret internationally. However, the education section currently lists only degrees, dates, and grades, so adding more detail about your academic journey would help a recruiter understand your background. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; for tailored advice, please specify your intended position or industry.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree in your education section**  
  Currently, your degrees list only the qualification, date, and grade. Add a line or two under each degree where you have relevant content: modules, thesis details, exchange semesters, certificates, scholarships, or mentoring programmes—only what is true and relevant to your future applications.
- 🔴 **Expand experience bullets to show skills and outcomes**  
  Most of your experience bullets describe tasks but not the skills you used or the results achieved. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, why it mattered, and what transferable skill it demonstrates. Add outcomes or figures where you can genuinely support them.
- 🟠 **Make bullet punctuation consistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it to every bullet for a professional and consistent appearance.
- 🟡 **Consider whether to show your Bachelor's grade**  
  Grades are optional on a CV and are commonly included from 5.00 upward. For grades below this, it is worth discussing with a Career Services coach whether to show them. The decision is yours.
