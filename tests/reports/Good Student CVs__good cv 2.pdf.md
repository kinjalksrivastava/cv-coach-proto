## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is in good shape overall, with a clear structure and a strong set of experiences for an early-career profile. However, there are a few structural issues to address: some entries at the top are not under a clear heading, and the Education section is missing or not labelled in a way that applicant tracking systems can read. There is currently no mention of AI experience or skills—if you have relevant coursework, projects, or tools in this area, consider adding them. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; for tailored advice, please share your target position or industry.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

ATS compatibility is estimated from three things this tool can actually observe: conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Entries at the top lack a section heading**  
  There is a block of entries at the top of your CV that sits under no heading. Open your file and check whether a heading is missing or unreadable—if so, add a clear section heading such as 'Education' to ensure both recruiters and applicant tracking systems can correctly interpret your academic background.
- 🔴 **Bullets in Work Experience describe duties, not your contribution**  
  Many of your work experience bullets focus on tasks rather than your specific contribution or the skills you demonstrated. Rework these to highlight what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on the transferable skills you developed.
- 🔴 **Most Work Experience bullets lack outcomes or skills shown**  
  Several bullets in your work experience section describe only the task, without showing the outcome or the skill behind it. For each, clarify what you achieved or what skill you demonstrated, and add results where you can genuinely support them.
- 🟠 **Paragraph at the top has no heading**  
  There is a paragraph at the top of your CV with no heading. If this is your profile or summary, add a heading such as 'Profile' and use it to briefly state what you bring and where you are heading.
- 🟡 **No Education section found**  
  Your CV does not currently have a clearly labelled Education section. Add one to ensure your academic background is easy to find.
