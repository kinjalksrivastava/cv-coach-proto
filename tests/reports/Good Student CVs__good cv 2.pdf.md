## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is well-structured in terms of length and includes clear role titles and descriptions for each work experience, which is a solid foundation. However, there are some structural issues: several entries at the top are missing a section heading, and there is no dedicated Education section, which makes it harder for a recruiter or applicant tracking system to interpret your academic background. Most of your work experience bullets focus on tasks rather than your specific contributions or the skills you demonstrated, so adding more detail here will help show your value. Language skills are listed without a proficiency level, which can make it difficult for a recruiter to assess your abilities. This feedback covers structure and completeness; if you have a specific target role or industry, more tailored advice is available.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from three things this tool can actually observe: conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Unheaded block of entries at the top**  
  Add a clear heading (such as 'Education') above the academic entries at the top of your CV. Without a heading, readers and applicant tracking systems may not recognise what these entries represent.
- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Revise your work experience bullets to highlight what you personally achieved, how you did it, and which transferable skills you demonstrated, rather than just listing tasks.
- 🔴 **Bullets lack outcomes or skills shown**  
  For each work experience bullet, clarify what changed as a result of your actions and which skills you used or developed. Add outcomes or results where you can genuinely support them.
- 🟠 **Paragraph at the top with no heading**  
  If the opening paragraph is your profile or summary, give it a heading so it is clearly identified. A profile should briefly state what you bring and where you are heading.
- 🟡 **No Education section found**  
  Add an 'Education' section to clearly present your academic background. This helps recruiters quickly find your qualifications.
