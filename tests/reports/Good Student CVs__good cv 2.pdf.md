### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Many of your work experience bullets list tasks rather than highlighting your personal contribution or the skills you developed. Rework these to focus on what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, emphasising transferable skills relevant to your future applications.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  Several experience bullets stop at describing the activity, without showing the outcome or the skill demonstrated. For each, clarify what you achieved, how you approached the task, and what skill it demonstrates—add results where you can genuinely evidence them.
- 🟠 **No Education section found**  
  Your CV does not have a dedicated Education section. If you have education worth showing, add a section with your degrees, institutions, dates, and any relevant details.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  Some grades are listed in a format that may not be clear to international recruiters. Specify the grading scale for each, and write grades as 'X / Y' so they are easily understood.
- 🟡 **Languages are listed without a level**  
  Add a proficiency level for each language (e.g., Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Fluent), and consider using CEFR levels (A1–C2) for clarity, as recruiters recognise these universally.
