## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is well-structured, fits on one page, and presents a clear timeline without gaps or overlaps. Each work experience entry includes both a role title and a description, and your section headings and dates are consistent throughout. However, there is a block of education entries at the top without a heading, which can confuse both readers and applicant tracking systems. Additionally, your work experience bullets focus mainly on tasks rather than your specific contributions or the skills you developed. There is currently no mention of AI experience anywhere in your CV—if you have relevant coursework, projects, or skills in this area, consider adding them. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; for tailored advice, please add a target position or industry.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add a heading to the education block at the top**  
  The entries for your university and high school currently sit under no heading. Add an 'Education' heading so readers and applicant tracking systems can clearly identify this section.
- 🔴 **Rework work experience bullets to show your contribution and skills**  
  Many of your work experience bullets describe duties rather than your specific contribution or the skills you used. Revise these to highlight what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on transferable skills.
- 🔴 **Add outcomes or skills to experience bullets**  
  Most of your experience bullets describe tasks without showing the outcome or the skill behind them. For each, clarify what changed as a result of your work or what skill you demonstrated.
- 🟠 **Add a heading to the opening paragraph if it is a profile**  
  If the paragraph at the top is your profile, give it a heading. A profile should briefly state what you bring and where you are heading.
- 🟡 **Add an Education section**  
  There is currently no 'Education' section. If you have education worth showing, add it with a clear heading.
