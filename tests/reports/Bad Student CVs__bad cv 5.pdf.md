ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **No bullet points anywhere in the CV**  
  Add bullet points under each role and activity to explain what you actually did, how you did it, and what changed as a result. Without these, a reader cannot assess your contributions or skills.
- 🔴 **Several sections are almost empty**  
  Expand each section—especially education and extracurriculars—with a few lines describing your responsibilities, achievements, and skills gained. This will help a reader understand your background and potential.
- 🔴 **The CV does not fill a page**  
  There is significant space to add more detail about your studies, activities, and any experience. A fuller page signals a more complete and competitive profile.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., '09/2023 – 06/2024') so a reader can accurately judge the duration of your studies and activities.
- 🟡 **No Experience section found**  
  If you have any paid work experience, internships, or student jobs, add a Work/Professional Experience section with details of your roles and what you accomplished.
