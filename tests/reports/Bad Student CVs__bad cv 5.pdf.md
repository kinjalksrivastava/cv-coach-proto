_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **No section headings could be read from this CV**  
  Add clear section headings as ordinary text in the body of your document (e.g., 'Education', 'Experience', 'Skills & Languages'). This ensures both recruiters and applicant tracking systems can navigate your CV easily.
- 🔴 **No bullet points anywhere in the CV**  
  For each role, activity, or achievement, add bullet points describing what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result. This gives readers a clear sense of your contributions and skills.
- 🔴 **The CV does not fill a page**  
  Expand on your academic, work, and extracurricular experiences to provide a fuller picture of your background. A student CV should make good use of the available space to highlight your strengths.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., '09/2019 – 06/2025') so readers can accurately assess the duration of your experiences.
- 🟡 **No Education section found**  
  Include a dedicated Education section listing your degrees, institutions, dates (with months), and any relevant coursework or achievements.
