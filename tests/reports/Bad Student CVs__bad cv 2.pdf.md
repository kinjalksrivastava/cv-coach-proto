### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Work experience entries have no description**  
  Add a few bullets under each work experience entry covering what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result—focus on the transferable skills that matter.
- 🔴 **The CV does not fill a page**  
  There is room to say considerably more about your achievements and activities. Expand on your roles, responsibilities, and outcomes to give a fuller picture of your background.
- 🟠 **Dates give the year but not the month**  
  Add the month to each date range (e.g., 'Sep 2023 – Jun 2024'). This helps a reader understand the duration of your experiences.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  Clarify your grade by showing the maximum possible (e.g., '513.00 / 6.00') so that recruiters from other countries can interpret it correctly.
- 🟡 **Grades are shown for some degrees but not others**  
  Recruiters may assume an omitted grade was weaker. Decide whether to show grades for all degrees or remove them entirely for consistency.
