### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Several bullets in your PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE section focus on tasks rather than your personal contribution or the skills you demonstrated. Rework these to highlight what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on transferable skills.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  Many experience bullets state what was done but not the skill or outcome. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and what skill it shows. Add results where you genuinely have them, but only if appropriate.
- 🟠 **Bullet punctuation is inconsistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it consistently throughout your CV.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE**  
  Tenses shift within your PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE section. Use past tense for completed roles and present tense for your current position, and keep this consistent within each entry.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  The grade for your Bachelor's degree is shown as '5' without a maximum. Write it as 5.00 / 6.00 so international recruiters can understand it.

- You have shown grades for some degrees but not others. Recruiters may assume an omitted grade was lower, so consider whether you want to add the missing grades or remove the others for consistency.
- If you have completed relevant certificates, such as the Certificate in Data Science Fundamentals (DSF) or the Certificate in Integrative Sustainability Management (SuM-HSG), you could add them here if they support your goals.
- If you participated in the HSG Mentoring Programme, you could add a bullet under the relevant degree, naming your mentor's industry if it is relevant.

> **On grades:** be aware that if you leave out grades for certain education experiences but not for others, recruiters will automatically assume that the missing grades were worse than the ones you have listed.
