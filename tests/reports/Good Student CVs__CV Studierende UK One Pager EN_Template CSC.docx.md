### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree, not just grades and dates**  
  For your Swiss Matura, add a line or two about relevant modules, special projects, or activities that relate to your future goals. This helps a recruiter see what you focused on and what you gained beyond the certificate itself.
- 🔴 **Experience bullets focus on tasks, not skills or outcomes**  
  Review each experience bullet and expand on what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered. Where possible, highlight the skills you developed and any results or changes that came from your work.
- 🟡 **Clarify grading scales for international readers**  
  For your BSc Economics, write the grade as 80/100 to make it clear. For your Swiss Matura, specify the grading scale (e.g., 5.0 out of 6.0) so that recruiters from other countries can understand your results.
- 🟡 **Add levels to your language and technical skills**  
  Add a proficiency level (e.g., Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Fluent) to each language and technical skill, and consider using CEFR levels (A1-C2) for languages for greater clarity.
- 🟡 **Give more detail for interests and activities**  
  For each interest or extracurricular activity, specify what you do, how often, and to what level. This gives a clearer picture of your commitment and achievements outside academics.
