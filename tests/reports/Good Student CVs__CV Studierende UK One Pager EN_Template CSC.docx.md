### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Some experience bullets only state the tasks performed, without highlighting your personal contribution or the skills demonstrated. Rework these to show what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on transferable skills.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  Most experience bullets describe what was done but not the skill or outcome. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and what skill it demonstrates. Add a result where you genuinely have one, but only if appropriate.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  Some grades are given in a way that may not be clear to international recruiters. Clarify the grading scale for your BSc and Swiss Matura so that readers from other countries can understand your results.
- 🟡 **Languages are listed without a level**  
  Add a proficiency level (e.g., Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Fluent) to each language, and consider using the CEFR scale (A1–C2) for clarity, as this is widely understood by recruiters.
- 🟡 **Interests are listed as single words**  
  Single-word interests are acceptable, but if one or two could say something interesting about you or show a skill, consider adding a brief detail. This is optional and not needed for every interest.

_Worked examples of weaker and stronger bullet points — Career Services' own, not rewrites of your CV — can be opened underneath this report._
