### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree, not just grades and dates**  
  For your Swiss Matura, add a line or two about relevant modules, a thesis, an exchange, or any additional qualification or scholarship, focusing on what is relevant to your future applications.
- 🔴 **Make experience bullets outcome-focused**  
  Review each experience bullet to clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, highlighting the transferable skills you developed and any concrete results where possible.
- 🟡 **Clarify your grades for international readers**  
  For your BSc Economics, write the grade as 80/100 so it is clear to all readers. For your Swiss Matura, specify which grading scale was used so recruiters outside Switzerland can interpret it correctly.
- 🟡 **Add levels to your skills and certificates**  
  For each skill and certificate, add a level (e.g., Basic, Intermediate, Advanced) and consider using CEFR levels for languages to ensure clarity for all recruiters.
- 🟡 **Add detail to your interests**  
  For each interest, specify what kind, how often, and to what level you pursue it, so a reader can see your commitment and engagement.
