## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is well-structured and covers a broad range of academic, professional, and extracurricular experiences, which gives a recruiter a good overview of your background. However, there are several areas where adding more detail or clarifying your contributions would make your achievements clearer and more compelling. Some sections are currently thin or missing key information, such as specific roles or outcomes, which can leave a reader with questions about your responsibilities and impact. The 'Interessen' section is almost empty, so consider either developing it or removing the heading. This review focuses on structure and completeness, as no target role or job description was provided. Additionally, there is currently no mention of AI experience in your CV—if you have relevant coursework, projects, or skills in this area, consider adding them.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree in EDUCATION**  
  Currently, some degrees only list the qualification and dates. Add a line or two under each degree where you have relevant content, such as key modules, thesis topic and what you did, exchange semesters, certificates, or scholarships.
- 🔴 **State your role titles in VOLUNTEERING, SOCIETIES & INTERESTS**  
  Some entries list only the organisation or event without your specific role. Always state the function you held so a reader can understand your level of responsibility.
- 🔴 **Develop or remove the almost empty 'Interessen' section**  
  A heading with almost nothing under it draws attention to the gap. Either add a few interests or remove the section entirely.
- 🟠 **Revise experience bullets to show skills and outcomes**  
  Most experience bullets describe tasks but not the skills used or the results achieved. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on transferable skills and outcomes where possible.
- 🟡 **Make bullet punctuation consistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it throughout your CV.
