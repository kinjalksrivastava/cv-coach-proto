## Your CV feedback report

Your CV is well-structured and covers a broad range of academic, professional, and extracurricular experiences, which gives a recruiter a clear sense of your background. The work experience section is particularly strong in providing both role titles and descriptions. However, there are a few areas where you can add detail or clarify your contributions to make your achievements stand out more clearly. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; for tailored advice, please specify your target position or industry.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Add detail under each degree in Education**  
  Currently, some degrees list only the title and dates. Add a line or two under each degree where you have relevant content: modules, thesis topic and what you did, exchange semesters, certificates, scholarships, or mentoring programmes—only what is true and relevant to your future applications.
- 🔴 **Develop or remove the nearly empty 'Interessen' section**  
  A section with only a heading and little or no content draws attention to a gap. Either add meaningful interests or remove the section entirely.
- 🟠 **Rework duty-based bullets in Work Experience**  
  Some bullets describe only tasks. Rework these to show what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on the transferable skills you demonstrated.
- 🟠 **Add outcomes or skills to experience bullets**  
  Most experience bullets describe tasks but not the skills used or the results achieved. For each, clarify what you did, how, and why it mattered, and add outcomes where you genuinely have them.
- 🟡 **Make bullet punctuation consistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it throughout for a professional appearance.

_Worked examples of weaker and stronger bullet points — Career Services' own, not rewrites of your CV — can be opened underneath this report._
