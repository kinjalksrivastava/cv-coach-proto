### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Several bullets in your Professional Experience section state only the tasks you performed, without showing your specific contribution or the skills you demonstrated. Rework these to highlight what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on the transferable skills you want to showcase.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  Many of your experience bullets list activities but do not show the outcome, skill, or change that resulted. For each, clarify what you did, how you did it, and what skill it demonstrates. Add a result where you genuinely have one, but only if it is appropriate and you can support it.
- 🟠 **Bullet punctuation is inconsistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it consistently throughout your CV.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE**  
  The tense shifts between past and present within your Professional Experience section. Use past tense for completed roles and present tense for your current position, and keep this consistent within each entry.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  The grade for your Bachelor's degree is shown as '5' without the maximum. Write it as 5.00 / 6.00 so that recruiters outside Switzerland can understand it.
