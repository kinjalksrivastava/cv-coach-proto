## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is in good shape overall, with a clear structure and a strong range of experiences for an early-stage student. Each work experience entry includes both a role title and a description, which helps a recruiter quickly understand your background. However, several of your experience bullets focus on tasks rather than the skills or impact behind them, so refining these will make your contributions stand out more. Your language skills and certifications are well presented. Nothing on your CV currently refers to AI, machine learning, or AI tools—if you have relevant experience or coursework in this area, consider adding it. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; if you add a target, I can give more tailored advice.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Rework your experience bullets to highlight what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered—especially the transferable skills you developed. Where possible, include a result or outcome, but only if you can genuinely support it.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  For each experience bullet, clarify what skill or competency it demonstrates. Add outcomes or changes where appropriate, but do not invent figures just to fill the gap.
- 🟠 **Bullet punctuation is inconsistent**  
  Choose a consistent approach to bullet punctuation—either end all bullets with a full stop or none—and apply it throughout your CV.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within "WORK EXPERIENCE"**  
  Use past tense for completed roles and present tense for current positions, and keep this consistent within the section.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  When listing your GPA, include the maximum (e.g., 5.04 / 6.00) so that recruiters from other countries can interpret it correctly.
