## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is in good shape overall, with a clear structure and a solid range of experiences for your stage. Each work experience entry includes both a role title and a description, which helps a recruiter quickly understand your background. However, several of your bullet points focus on tasks rather than the skills you developed or the impact you made, so adding more detail about your contributions and outcomes will make your CV stand out further. Nothing on your CV currently mentions experience with AI tools or projects—if you have any relevant exposure, consider adding it. As you have not provided a target role or job description, this feedback focuses on structure and completeness; if you add a target, I can give more tailored advice.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **Bullets describe duties rather than contribution**  
  Several bullets in your work experience section list only the tasks you performed, without showing what you contributed or which skills you developed. Rework these to highlight what you did, how you did it, and why it mattered, focusing on the transferable skills you gained.
- 🔴 **Most bullets describe the task, not the skill behind it**  
  Many experience bullets stop at describing the activity, without mentioning the skill demonstrated or any outcome. For each, clarify what skill you used or developed, and add a result where you genuinely have one.
- 🟠 **Bullet punctuation is inconsistent**  
  Some bullets end with a full stop and others do not. Choose one style and apply it consistently throughout your CV.
- 🟠 **Bullet tense changes within "WORK EXPERIENCE"**  
  The tense shifts between present and past within your work experience section. Use past tense for completed roles and present tense for current positions, and keep this consistent within each entry.
- 🟡 **Grades need clarifying**  
  Your St. Gallen GPA is listed as 5.04, but without the maximum scale. Write it as 5.04 / 6.00 so international recruiters can interpret it correctly.
