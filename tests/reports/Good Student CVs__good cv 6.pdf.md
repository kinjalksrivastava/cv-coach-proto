## Your CV feedback report

### 1. Overall impression

Your CV is well-structured, fits neatly on one page, and presents your academic and professional background clearly, with consistent headings and dates. Grades are shown with their maximum, making them easy to interpret internationally, and your skills are listed without unsupported trait words. However, there are currently no bullet points under any of your roles or activities, which means a recruiter cannot see what you actually did in each position. This feedback focuses on structure and completeness, as no target role or job description was provided. Nothing on your CV currently mentions AI experience—if you have relevant coursework, projects, or skills in this area, consider adding them.

_Please note that this is a first feedback, mainly about the structure and content of your CV. It does not evaluate in detail how well you have tailored it to a particular role, and it is not a judgement about you as a candidate. That is what your one-on-one session with Career Services is for._

_An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software employers use to collect, scan and process applications digitally — many CVs are read by one before a person sees them. ATS compatibility is estimated from four things this tool can actually observe: standard fonts, conventional section headings, ordinary bullet characters, and no table- or image-based layout. It is an indication, not a guarantee — every applicant tracking system parses differently._

### 3. Key areas to improve

- 🔴 **No bullet points anywhere in the CV**  
  Every role and activity needs a few lines saying what was actually done. Without them, a reader has only job titles to go on. Add bullet points under each entry in your Education, Work Experience, and Extracurriculars sections to describe your contributions, skills used, and outcomes achieved.

_Worked examples of weaker and stronger bullet points — Career Services' own, not rewrites of your CV — can be opened underneath this report._

### 6. Anything else from HSG worth adding?

Before your appointment, it is worth asking yourself whether there is anything else you have done at HSG that belongs on this CV: a club, a certificate, a mentoring programme, a competition, a project with a company. A few examples of what is on offer are below. They are examples, not recommendations, and only what is genuinely relevant to the role you are applying for is worth adding.

- **[HSG Mentoring Programme](https://www.unisg.ch/en/studying/starting-your-studies/counselling-services/mentoring-programme/)** — two-year one-to-one mentoring, matched with an experienced professional
  _Belongs in: Education, as a bullet under the degree (name the mentor's industry if relevant)_
- **[Bachelor's / Master's thesis](https://www.unisg.ch/)** — the academic capstone; several institutes welcome theses written with a company
  _Belongs in: Education_
- **[Capstone Project (BWL)](https://www.unisg.ch/en/studying/programmes/bachelor/major-in-business-administration-bwl/curriculum/)** — a semester acting as business consultants for a real practice partner
  _Belongs in: Education_
- **Talents Conference (Career Services)** — HSG's biggest careers fair, student-organised, with paid part-time roles and day volunteers
  _Belongs in: a paid part-time role: Work Experience. Volunteering on the day: Volunteering or Interests_
- **[My HSG Career](https://my.hsgcareer.ch)** — part-time jobs, internships and company events advertised for HSG students
  _Belongs in: Reference, not a CV section_
