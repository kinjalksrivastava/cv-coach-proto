### 2. Cover letter essentials

| Check | Brief feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Adds value beyond the CV | You include specific new details and personal insights that would not be obvious from a CV alone. |
| Why this organisation? | You explain your interest in Modern Technology AG by referencing their innovative use of AI and your positive impression from direct interaction with their technology. |
| Why this role? | You state clearly why the Graduate Business Innovation position appeals to you, especially its focus on impactful AI projects and dynamic work. |
| What you bring | You connect your technical background, adaptability, and stakeholder engagement experience directly to the demands of the position. |
| Conclusion | You close with a confident statement of your potential contribution and express interest in interviewing. |
| Company / recipient details | The company and recipient details are there. |
| Job title / reference number | The role and a reference number are both named. |
| Length: max. one page | Your letter is one page, which is the maximum. Around two-thirds of a page of substance is usually enough. |
| Availability / start date | Your availability or start date is mentioned. |
| Previous contact with the organisation | You mention contact you have already had with the organisation — that belongs in a cover letter, so keep it. |
