### 2. Cover letter essentials

| Check | Brief feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Adds value beyond the CV | The letter mostly summarizes past roles and achievements in narrative form, and without the CV, I cannot confirm if it adds significant new information. |
| Why this organisation? | There is no specific reason given for wanting to join Awesome Engineering Company in particular. |

| Length: max. one page | The length could not be measured from what you uploaded, so please check it yourself. One page is the maximum, and around two-thirds of a page of substance is usually enough. |
