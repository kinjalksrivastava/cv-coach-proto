## Your cover letter feedback

### 1. Overall assessment

Your letter provides a clear overview of your background and connects your experience to the R & D Engineer role, but it mainly focuses on describing your past roles and achievements. While you mention your motivation and some alignment with the position, the letter could do more to explain why you are interested in this specific company and what draws you to this particular role beyond your general fit.

**Paragraphs that read like a list of past experiences**

You did not upload your CV, so nothing here has been compared with one. Each of these reads as a list of things you have done, which is what a CV is for — so it may not add much beyond one.

- “Prior to CMU, I spent two years working on product research and development at N…” — This paragraph mainly lists your previous responsibilities and achievements at National Engineering, Inc. and may not add much beyond what a CV would already show; it could benefit from more context about your approach, decisions, or what you learned.

_Please note that this is only a first feedback mainly about the structure and value-add of your Cover Letter. It does not evaluate the content in detail or how well you tailored the Cover Letter to the role. This you can discuss in your one-on-one session._

### 2. Cover letter essentials

| Check | Brief feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Adds value beyond the CV | The letter gives some context about your project leadership and problem-solving approach, but much of it reads as a summary of a CV. |
| Why this organisation? | There is no specific explanation of why you want to work for this company in particular. |
| Why this role? | You mention your enjoyment of research and problem solving, but do not elaborate on what specifically interests you about this R & D Engineer position. |
| What you bring | You connect your experience in product design and development to the role, but could strengthen the link by explaining how your skills or insights would contribute to this company's projects. |
| Conclusion | You close with a polite thank you and an expression of interest, which is appropriate. |
| Company / recipient details | No company or recipient block found — add who the letter is to and where. We don't check whether the details are correct. |
| Job title / reference number | The role is named. If the advert carries a reference number, add that too. |
| Length: max. one page | The length could not be measured from what you uploaded, so please check it yourself. One page is the maximum, and around two-thirds of a page of substance is usually enough. |
| Availability / start date | No start date mentioned. Recruiters often find it helpful to know when you could start — useful rather than required. |
| Previous contact with the organisation | Have you already spoken to someone at the organisation or attended one of their events? If so, consider mentioning this in your explanation of why the organisation interests you. |
